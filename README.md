## 🔬 Executive Overview

I work at the intersection of life sciences, data engineering, and applied AI. With a Ph.D. in evolutionary genomics and 6+ years of research and development experience, my core strength lies in rapidly structuring ambiguous problems, translating technical complexity across disciplines, and engineering robust solutions from the ground up.

* **High-Throughput Omics & HPC:** End-to-end orchestration of large-scale Whole-Genome Sequencing (WGS) and RNA-Seq pipelines on institutional SLURM clusters with containerized reproducibility.
* **Developer Tools & Desktop Applications:** Creator of practical, open-source software born directly from research bottlenecks:
  * [📚 BioReader](https://github.com/BurakBulut-sh/BioReader): Intelligent scientific PDF reader and literature workspace with visual monograph layout and offline biological glossary.
  * [🔍 SampleSheriff](https://github.com/BurakBulut-sh/samplesheriff): Fast, strict pre-flight sample sheet and metadata validator for bioinformatics pipelines.
  * [⏱️ PipeWatch](https://github.com/BurakBulut-sh/pipewatch): Lightweight pipeline observability and resource telemetry tool for SLURM/HPC workflows.
* **Applied AI & Autonomous Agents:** Designing on-premise, privacy-preserving LLM agents (llama.cpp, HuggingFace, Hermes Agent) for literature reasoning, telemetry auditing, and code synthesis.
